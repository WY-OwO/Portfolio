Factory Reset

Factory Reset is a 2D pixel game developed by the second-year graduate students from Northeastern University, Oakland, as their capstone project. I volunteered to be one of their two artists.

Given the document describing the aesthetics and several other information about the visual goal and needed assets, I'm heavily inspired by the top-down 2D pixel game, Baba Is You, and the tone of Portals.

My Roles:

Character artist and animator, UI artist.
